# DSC-563
This repository includes data/visualizations for DSC 563 Fall 2026
The visualization asks, "What is the workshop attendance of each neighborhood in October 2026?" The intended audience is the community workshop coordinator.
The simulated table and starter code may be reused under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. External software and readings retain their own licenses.

Files and descriptions:
Module_01_Audience_First_Chart_DSC563_Polivka.ipunb- Associated code
m01_claim_check.csv- Spreadsheet of attendance and seating capacity of each neighborhood in the month of October
tableu_workshops_tidy.csv- Spreadsheet of attendance and seating capacity of each neighborhood in the months of May through October
m01_after_chart.png- Image of a bar chart of attendance by neighborhood for the month of October from Colab notebook
DSC_Oct_Attendance_Visits.png- Image of a bar chart of attendance by neighborhood for the month of October from Colab notebook with cleaned labels and color correction 
Tableu_Chart_Module2_DSC563.png- Image of a bar chart of attendance by neighborhood for the month of October from Tableu

Follow the following link https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1OV0opDql-Q_5x_YujTVGa2x1lHYFi-_6?usp=sharing to open notebook. Click "Run All" to run code and view outputs. 
Tableu_Chart_Module2_DSC563.png is a bar chart created in Tableu of attendance in each neighborhood in the month of October 2026. No filters applied to neighborhood row. Month column filtered to include only October. Attendance label modified to reflect the name "Attendance (seat capacity" and chart title added as "October 2026 attendance ranged from 82 to 94 visits". Units of attendance are seat visit instances with a tick interval of 10.
